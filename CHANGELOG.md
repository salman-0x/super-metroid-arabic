# Changelog

Found by testing with an all-items save file: two texts that were still English are now Arabic.
- Equipment screen: the reserve-tank box ("MODE [AUTO]" / "MANUAL" / "RESERVE TANK").
- The title "PLANET ZEBES" of the map screen shown after loading a save file.
Census: 99.6 % of the counted text volume (1997 of 2006 characters). Patch result SHA-1 `27758c9ce3075411f10c34b1bb9a72f6145dc812`.

(labels, 14 item names, map buttons, 7 area names), HUD. Not translated by design: title logo, ending lines, staff credits, L/R, Japanese text mode.
